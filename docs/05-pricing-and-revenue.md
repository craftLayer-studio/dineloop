# 05 — Pricing and revenue

USD equivalents use ₹88 = $1 (estimate, late-2026 rate; recheck before publishing). All prices exclude 18% GST.

## Pricing principles

1. **Per outlet, flat, no commission.** Unlimited tables, orders and staff. No commission on dine-in or direct orders. This is the opposite of aggregators and of Thrive's 3%.
2. **Anchor under Petpooja's bundles.** Petpooja charges ₹20,000 for its ordering bundle and ₹10,000–30,000 for its marketing bundle ([petpooja.com/pricing](https://petpooja.com/pricing), accessed 2026-11-24). DineLoop Growth bundles both jobs for ₹24,999.
3. **Annual by default, monthly allowed.** Annual = 2 months free. Annual prepay funds CAC and lowers churn.
4. **Pass-through costs at cost plus margin.** WhatsApp messages and hardware are not hidden in the plan price.
5. **Free pilot, not a free plan.** 14-day free pilot with real service; then pay. No freemium tier in India year one (support cost too high for free users).

| Hardware refresh | KDS tablet, printers | Low margin, high stickiness |

## Discount rules

- Pilot to paid within 14 days: setup fee waived.
- NRAI/local association members: 10% off annual.
- Referral: referring owner gets 1 month free per paid referral; referred owner gets setup free.
- Never discount Growth below ₹19,999/year (protects anchor).
