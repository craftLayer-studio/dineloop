## Trends 2024–2026

1. **Aggregator backlash is escalating.** Restaurants paused Zomato/Swiggy orders from 1 July 2025 over commissions and hidden charges ([YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2025/07/zomato-swiggy-vs-restaurants-online-food-delivery-ondc-rapido)); Bengaluru's hotel association threatened a boycott from 15 August 2026 ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/india/story/bengaluru-restaurants-to-boycott-swiggy-zomato-from-august-15-heres-why-545884-2026-07-29)); NRAI asked CCI to stop Zomato's price-parity clauses (July 2026) ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/zomato-price-parity-cci-case-why-nrai-oppose-explained-126071500578_1.html)). Owners actively want direct channels.
2. **Zomato's active restaurant base fell** for the first time (344,000 to 328,000, Q1 FY27), partly from delistings ([Lapaas Voice](https://lapaasvoice.com/zomatos-active-restaurant-count-fall-for-first-time-in-q1-fy27/)).
3. **Staff shortage.** ~60% of operators report kitchen and service staff shortages; 94% plan Tier 2–3 expansion ([IANS via Lokmat](https://www.lokmattimes.com/national/94-pc-of-indian-restaurant-operators-plan-to-expand-in-tier-ii-iii-cities-report)). Self-ordering directly offsets this.
4. **WhatsApp moved to per-message pricing** (1 July 2025). India marketing rose from ~₹0.78 to ₹0.8631 per message in 2026; utility/authentication ₹0.115; service messages chargeable from 1 October 2026 beyond 1,000 free per number per month ([MyOperator](https://myoperator.com/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india-2026), [Meta](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing)). Utility bills are cheap; marketing must be targeted.
5. **QR ordering lifts order size** in vendor data: Mr Yum ~30% (sponsored), Lightspeed 25%, Flipdish case 25%, others ~12% ([Lightspeed](https://www.lightspeedhq.com/au/blog/qr-table-ordering-growth-and-trends/), [Flipdish](https://www.flipdish.com/gb/customers/samsi-manchester)). Treat as marketing-grade; we must measure our own.
6. **Restaurant tech funding continues.** Petpooja raised a $15.5M Series C in 2025 ([Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/news/petpooja-raises-155-mn-series-c-at-over-3x-valuation-premium-10512396)); Owner.com scaled on direct ordering in the US.
7. **AI in restaurant software.** Petpooja now bundles an "AI agent" and "AI marketing agent" in plans ([petpooja.com/pricing](https://petpooja.com/pricing)). AI insights are becoming table stakes for P2.
