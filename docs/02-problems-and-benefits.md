# 02 — Problems and benefits

## Staff (waiters, captains, kitchen)

| Problem | Solution | Benefit | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| Running for menus, orders and bills all night | Diners self-order and self-pay | Fewer steps; serve more tables | Retention |

## Benefit summary for the sales pitch

For a 20-table cafe with ₹12 lakh monthly revenue (estimate), Growth at ₹2,499/month pays back if it delivers any one of:

- 7 extra ₹1,000 visits a month from WhatsApp campaigns (estimate: ₹2,499 / ~₹350 gross margin per visit), or
- moving 15 delivery orders a month from Zomato to direct ordering (saves ~₹200 each), or
- a 1% lift in AOV (₹12,000/month extra revenue).

The pitch is "pays for itself with one table a week".
